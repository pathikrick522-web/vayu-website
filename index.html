// 01 WHO WE ARE

THE AEROSPACE INITIATIVE

Founded at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata, Team VAYU is a multidisciplinary student engineering team dedicated to conceptualizing, engineering, and piloting high-performance unmanned aerial vehicles.

Inspired by Vayu — the eternal element of wind and motion — we fuse computational fluid dynamics, embedded avionics, lightweight composite structures, and autonomous flight controls. From theoretical calculations on paper to wind tunnel analyses and full-scale airfield flight testing, our members apply rigorous engineering principles to conquer complex aerospace challenges.
