Competitions

SUAS International

The Student Unmanned Aerial Systems (SUAS) competition fosters interest in UAS technologies, challenging teams to design autonomous systems capable of executing complex missions including surveillance, object detection, and payload delivery in real-world scenarios.

Read More →

SAE International International

SAE Aero Design includes regional competitions that challenge participating teams to conceive, design, fabricate, and test a radio controlled aircraft that can take off, land, and optimally meet the mission requirements with maximum payload capacity.

Read More →

IARC International

The International Aerial Robotics Competition (IARC) is the world's longest-running collegiate challenge that advances state-of-the-art aerial robotics by tasking students with solving "impossible" missions in a collaborative, industry-recognized environment.

Read More →

Aerothon National

Aerothon, organized by SAE India, is a national-level competition that challenges students to design and innovate Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) for solving real-world problems through advanced aerodynamics and structural engineering.

Read More →

MICRO DDC National

In the Micro Class category of the SAE Drone Development Challenge (DDC), students design aircraft that can carry heavy payloads while fitting within a 3-cubic-foot box, pushing the limits of compact aerodynamic design.

Read More →

REGULAR DDC National

The SAE DDC Regular Class challenges students to optimize aerodynamics while carrying significant payloads within strict design constraints and weight limits, testing complete aircraft engineering skills.

Read More →

About Us

We are Team Vayu — named after the Hindu deity of wind, embodying the spirit of flight and the relentless pursuit of aerial excellence.

Our multi-disciplinary team of engineering students spans aerodynamics, avionics, software engineering, and autonomous systems — all united by a single mission: to push the boundaries of what's possible in unmanned aerial vehicles.

From custom-built racing quadcopters to autonomous fixed-wing aircraft, we engineer solutions that compete on the world stage and inspire the next generation of aerospace innovators.

Aerodynamics

Airframe design, CFD simulation, and wind tunnel testing

Avionics

Flight controllers, sensor fusion, PCB design & power systems

Software & AI

ROS-based autonomy, computer vision, SLAM & ML systems

Structures

Composites, 3D printing, CNC machining & structural analysis

Ground Station

Telemetry, real-time data, mission planning & comms

Operations

Sponsorship, events, content creation & brand strategy
